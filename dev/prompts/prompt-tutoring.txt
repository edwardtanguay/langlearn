For each section, write a title and a short description of about 300 words in B1/B2 French. Before the text, make a drag/drop exercise where the learner can drag the 5 most difficult vocabulary words in the text to their definitions. After the text, write one general opinion question about the topic of the section that the teacher and the student can both answer and discuss.
